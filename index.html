05 / WORKFLOW

How I manage the moving parts.

I keep the brief, people, content, and deadlines aligned so everyone knows what needs to happen next.

01

Get the brief clear.

Before turning a brief into creator communication, I need to know what actually matters: the objective, message, deliverables, timeline, and non-negotiables.

02

Keep the creator's voice.

The key messages and deliverables stay clear, while the content still fits the creator's style.

03

Follow up on the details.

I track pending information, feedback, revisions, and approvals before they turn into delays.

04

Align the people involved.

I help keep the brand, creator, and internal team aligned on the same brief and expectations.

05

Close the loop.

I check that deliverables are live, links and proof are collected, and reporting is ready.
